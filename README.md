# Kiarash Kasbi

**MSc Medical Biotechnology | Computational Pathology | Whole-Slide Image Analysis | 3D Tissue Reconstruction**

I am an MSc Medical Biotechnology student at the University of Padua, moving from a biology-first background toward computational biomedical research.

My current thesis direction combines **whole-slide histology image registration** with downstream **3D tissue reconstruction** and **biomedical visualization** of serial tissue sections. The work connects tissue-section alignment, deformation-aware quality control, high-resolution image export, and reconstruction-oriented visualization.

I am especially interested in research at the intersection of tissue biology, medical images, computational workflows, spatial reconstruction, and visual quality control.
